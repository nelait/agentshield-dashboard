1. Executive Summary

AgentShield occupies a unique position in the AI governance market: it is an "Agent Governance Firewall" — a transparent runtime proxy that enforces policy, budget, compliance, and guardrails on every AI agent call without modifying the agents themselves.

Every competitor approaches AI governance differently. Here's how they cluster:

Category Competitors AgentShield Differentiator

Governance, Risk, Compliance		 Credo AI, OneTrust, Holistic AI These are documentation & assessment tools. AgentShield enforces policy at the wire level — every request is blocked or allowed in real time.
Model Lifecycle Platforms

Data Security Platforms

None of these competitors provide runtime agent-level governance. They govern models, data, or documentation — AgentShield governs the live call path of autonomous agents. This is the fundamental differentiator in every conversation.

11. Sales Battlecard — Handling Objections

🗣️ Common Objections & Responses

"We already have Credo AI / OneTrust for AI governance."
Great — those handle documentation and risk assessment. But when an agent tries to call a restricted endpoint at 2 AM, who blocks it? Credo will log it in a risk register tomorrow. AgentShield returns a 403 in 5 milliseconds. We're the enforcement layer your governance platform is missing.
"IBM watsonx covers governance and explainability."
watsonx is excellent for model lifecycle — training, lineage, explainability. But agents aren't models — they're autonomous systems calling 5 different APIs per request. watsonx can't enforce a budget limit, block prompt injection, or proxy MCP traffic. We govern the agent, not just the model inside it.
"We're a Microsoft shop — Purview handles our AI governance."
Purview protects your data from AI (DLP, sensitivity labels). AgentShield protects your organization from agent behavior — who can call which agent, how much they can spend, what content is allowed. Different attack surface, different product. They're complementary.
"AgentShield is unknown — these are established vendors."
Those vendors have been governing models for years. Agent governance is a new category — none of them have a runtime agent firewall because they weren't designed for agentic architectures. We're purpose-built for MCP/A2A/REST agents. That's why there's no checkbox for "agent gateway" in their feature lists.
"Self-hosted sounds like a lot of operational burden."
It's a single Docker Compose with PostgreSQL. No Kubernetes required for initial deployment. Your data never leaves your network — critical for HIPAA/SOX. And there's no $300K annual license renewal. Total cost of ownership is a fraction of any SaaS competitor.
"You don't have EU AI Act compliance templates."
Correct — and that's our roadmap. But consider this: EU AI Act says high-risk systems require human oversight and risk mitigation measures. Our HITL review, per-agent guardrails, and policy enforcement are exactly those measures. We implement the technical controls the regulation requires — we just don't generate the paperwork yet. Pair us with Credo/OneTrust for the documentation layer.
"Do you use open standards or is this all proprietary?"
We're fully committed to open standards. OPA/Rego (CNCF graduated) for policy-as-code — the same policy engine used by Kubernetes and Terraform. NIST OSCAL for compliance — import any OSCAL catalog (SOX, HIPAA, NIST 800-53, FedRAMP) and export machine-readable assessment results. No other AI governance platform supports both. Your policies are portable, your compliance artifacts are auditor-ready, and there's zero lock-in.
